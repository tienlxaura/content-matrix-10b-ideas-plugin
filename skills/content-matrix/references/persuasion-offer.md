# Thuyet phuc, Offer & Email

Tong hop tu: copywriting, offers, emails, cold-email. Dung khi viet landing, sales page, CTA, offer, email sequence, cold outreach.

## 1. Copywriting Principles — nen cua moi cau chu

- Clarity Over Cleverness: chon ro rang truoc hai huoc. Ro rang convert cao hon (+81% conversions trong nghien cuu tong hop). Doc phai decode la thua.
- Benefits Over Features: feature la no lam gi; benefit la dieu do co nghia gi cho khach. Moi feature them "which means..." de bac cau sang benefit.
- Specificity Over Vagueness: "cat reporting tu 4h ve 15 phut" thang "save time".
- Customer Language Over Company Language: dung tu khach dung (lay tu research-voice). Mirror VOC.
- One Idea Per Section: moi section 1 luan diem, xep logic tu tren xuong.

Style rules: simple over complex (use not utilize), specific over vague (cam streamline/optimize/innovative rong), active over passive, confident over qualified (bo almost/very/really), show over tell (ta outcome thay vi adverb), honest over sensational (khong bia stat/testimonial — legal liability).

Be Direct: vao thang diem. Dung rhetorical questions ("Tired of chasing approvals?"), analogy khi can, humor chi khi hop brand va khong lam mo clarity.

## 2. Page Structure — hero + core sections

Hero: Headline (1 message quan trong nhat, specific > generic; formulas: Achieve outcome without pain / The category for audience / Never unpleasant event again / Question highlighting pain; viet duoi dang transformation discomfort → vision → path + test "Now you can") + Subheadline (mo rong, them cu the, 1–2 cau) + Primary CTA (action + what they get: "Start Free Trial" hon "Sign Up").

Core sections: Social Proof (logos, stats, testimonials) → Problem/Pain (cho thay hieu ho) → Solution/Benefits (3–5 benefits noi outcome) → How It Works (3–4 buoc giam perceived complexity) → Objection Handling (FAQ, comparison, guarantee) → Final CTA (recap value + CTA + risk reversal).

Page-specific: Homepage phuc vu nhieu audience nhung khong chung chung, dan luong theo intent. Landing 1 message 1 CTA, headline khop ad/traffic source. Pricing giup chon goi, recommended plan ro. Feature noi feature → benefit → outcome + use cases. About ke tai sao ton tai + noi ve benefit khach + van co CTA.

Voice/Tone: chot formality (casual / professional-friendly / formal-enterprise) + personality (playful-serious, bold-understated, technical-accessible). Headline duoc bold hon, body phai clear hon, CTA action-oriented.

Output: Page Copy theo section + Annotations (vi sao chon, ap dung nguyen tac gi) + Alternatives (2–3 options cho headline/CTA kem rationale) + Meta (title/description neu can).

## 3. CTA Formula

Cong thuc: Action Verb + What They Get (+ Qualifier). "Start My Free Trial", "Get the Complete Checklist", "See Pricing for My Team". Tranh Submit/Sign Up/Learn More/Click Here/Get Started chung chung.

## 4. Offer — sua offer truoc khi sua copy

Offer la thu ban, khong phai trang ban. Copy hay cho offer yeu chi cong don cham. Offer manh voi copy trung binh convert ngay. Hau het "can copy hay hon" thuc ra la "can offer manh hon".

Value Equation: Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice). Tang dream outcome (noi vao muc tieu lon dang sau), tang perceived likelihood (proof, guarantee, methodology cu the), giam time delay (onboarding nhanh, win dau nhanh), giam effort (done-for-you, it quyet dinh hon, de hoc hon). Cham 4 levers 1–10, diem thap nhat la nut that.

Anatomy 6 thanh phan: Core deliverable (duoc gi) + Bonus stack (them gi khien core thay re — additive, khong inflation $50K bonus cho sp $497) + Guarantee (sao neu khong work — chon dung loai theo business model, khong over-promise) + Scarcity/Urgency (sao mua ngay — chi real) + Name (ten goi duoc) + Price + payment structure.

Diagnostic loop: xac dinh business type (service/course/coaching/info/high-ticket B2B/direct-response; SaaS self-serve thi pricing tiers quan trong hon) → viet offer hien tai bang plain language → chay value equation → audit anatomy thieu gi → sua 1 lever/iteration → project lift that (1 component 10–40%; 2 iterations stack 2–3x; ai hua 5x la ban hang).

Banned vocabulary khi draft offer: game-changing, revolutionary, disruptive, next-level, 10x, secret/hidden, limited time khong co deadline that, worth $X khong co comparable, 100% guaranteed khong dieu kien. Thay bang so cu the, khach co ten, outcome, timeline that.

## 5. Email Sequence — lifecycle/nurture

Nguyen tac: One Email One Job (1 muc dich + 1 CTA chinh), Value Before Ask, Relevance Over Volume, Clear Path Forward.

Do dai: Welcome 3–7, Nurture 5–10, Onboarding 5–10, Re-engagement 3–5. Timing: welcome ngay lap tuc; dau sequence 1–2 ngay; nurture 2–4 ngay; long-term weekly/bi-weekly. B2B tranh weekend.

Subject: Clear > Clever, Specific > Vague, benefit/curiosity, 40–60 ky tu. Patterns: Question ("Still struggling with X?"), How-to, Number ("3 ways to benefit"), Direct ("[Name], your thing is ready"), Story tease. Preview 90–140 ky tu, noi tiep subject chu khong lap lai.

Cac loai chinh:
- Welcome (5–7 emails/12–14 ngay): deliver value → quick win → story/why → social proof → objection → feature highlight → conversion.
- Nurture (6–8/2–3 tuan): lead magnet → expand → problem deep-dive → solution framework → case study → differentiation → objection → direct offer.
- Re-engagement (3–4/2 tuan, trigger 30–60 ngay inactive): check-in → value reminder → incentive → last chance.
- Onboarding (5–7/14 ngay, ho tro khong trung in-app): welcome + first step → getting started → feature → success story → check-in → advanced tip → upgrade.

Copy: Hook → Context → Value → CTA → Sign-off human. Format ngan (1–3 cau/doan, whitespace, bullet, bold tiet che, mobile-first). Do dai: transactional 50–125 tu, educational 150–300, story 300–500. CTA button cho primary, link cho secondary.

Output: Sequence Overview (name, trigger, goal, length, timing, exit) + tung Email (send, subject, preview, body full, CTA → destination, segment) + Metrics Plan.

## 6. Cold Email — outbound

Voice: peer thong minh vua thay dieu relevant, khong phai vendor. Conversational nhung gon, confident khong pushy. C-suite ultra-brief peer-level; mid-level them value cu the; technical precise no fluff. Cam template doi field, cam pitch deck nen thanh doan van, cam "I hope this finds you well", "I came across your profile", leverage/synergy/best-in-class.

5 nguyen tac: peer not vendor (contractions, doc to thay marketing la viet lai) / moi cau phai earn place (cat cau khong day ve reply) / personalization phai noi vao problem (bo doan personalize ma email van dung nghia la hong) / lead voi their world (you/your ap dao I/we, khong mo bang toi la ai) / one ask low friction (interest-based "Worth exploring?" thang meeting request, 1 CTA, reply 1 dong la xong).

Cau truc chon theo tinh huong: Observation → Problem → Proof → Ask / Question → Value → Ask / Trigger → Insight → Ask / Story → Bridge → Ask.

Subject cold: 2–4 tu, lowercase, khong punctuation trick, trong nhu dong nghiep ("reply rates", "hiring ops"). Khong pitch, khong urgency, khong emoji, khong first name.

Follow-up 3–5 emails, gap tang dan, moi email them goc/proof/resource moi (cam "just checking in"), moi email dung doc lap, breakup email ton trong last touch.
