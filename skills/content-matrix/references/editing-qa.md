# Editing QA — 7 Sweeps + Hoi dong + Quick-pass

Tong hop tu copy-editing. Dung truoc khi giao moi bai (SKILL.md §6). Nguyen tac: khong doi core message, chi nang no len. Nhieu pass hep hon 1 review lan man. Moi edit co ly do. Giu voice tac gia.

## 1. Seven Sweeps — chay tuan tu, vong lai sau moi sweep

### Sweep 1: Clarity — doc hieu khong?
Check: cau roi, dai tu mo ho, jargon, phat bieu mo ho, thieu context. Killers: 1 cau nhoi nhieu y, tru tuong thay cu the, gia dinh reader biet thu ho khong biet, chon diem trong qualifications. Process: doc nhanh highlight cho mo → roi moi sua → verify giu intent. Xong check Rule of One (1 y/section) + You Rule (viet cho reader).

### Sweep 2: Voice & Tone — deu giong khong?
Check: formal/casual lan lon, personality luc nay luc khac, mood gay, tu lech brand. Loi: mo casual ket corporate, luc "we" luc "the company", humor lac cho, technical roi rac. Process: doc to de nghe cho ven → danh dau → sua transition. Xong vong lai Clarity.

### Sweep 3: So What — moi claim tra loi "thi sao?"
Check: feature khong benefit, claim khong he qua, cau khong noi doi reader. Test: moi cau hoi "Okay, so what?" Khong tra loi duoc thi them cau noi "which means...". Vi du: "AI analytics" → "surface insights bo lo thu cong — quyet tot hon trong nua thoi gian". Xong vong lai Voice → Clarity.

### Sweep 4: Prove It — moi claim co bang chung?
Check: claim khong proof, thieu social proof, "best/leading" khong nguon. Proof can: testimonial co ten cu the, case ref, stat/data, third-party validation, guarantee/risk reversal, logo, review score. Gap: "trusted by thousands" (ai?), "customers love us" (cho thay noi). Process: list claim can proof → check proof gan do → flag unsupported → them proof hoac lam mem claim. Xong vong lai So What → Voice → Clarity.

### Sweep 5: Specificity — du that de tin?
Check: vague (improve/enhance/optimize), chung chung ai cung noi duoc, so tron cam giac bia, thieu detail that. Upgrades: save time → save 4h/tuan; many customers → 2,847 teams; fast → 14 days; great support → response trong 2h. Khong cu the hoa duoc thi do la filler — cat. So chi dung khi co that; khong co so that thi dung chi tiet giac quan. Xong vong lai Prove It → So What → Voice → Clarity.

### Sweep 6: Emotion — co cam thay gi?
Check: flat informational, trigger thieu, pain noi ma khong tham, aspiration noi ma khong goi. Chieu: pain hien tai, frustration voi alternative, FOMO, desire transformation, pride chon dung, relief. Ky thuat: ve before vivid, sensory language, micro-story, shared experience, cau hoi phan chieu. Emotion phuc vu message, khong thao tung. Xong vong lai tu Specificity xuong Clarity.

### Sweep 7: Zero Risk — het rao can hanh dong?
Check: friction gan CTA, objection chua tra loi, thieu trust signal, next step mo, chi phi an. Reducers: money-back, trial, no credit card, cancel anytime, proof gan CTA, noi ro dieu gi xay ra tiep, privacy assurance. Loi: CTA doi commitment khi chua earn trust, fine print gay nghi, "Contact us" mo. Xong vong lai full tu Emotion ve Clarity 1 vong cuoi.

## 2. Expert Panel Scoring — cua them cho bai quan trong

Bat buoc cho launch copy, pricing, landing traffic cao. Khuyen dung cho email sequence, sales page, ad copy. Optional cho blog/social. Skip cho update nho.

Lap 3–5 personas dung loai bai:
- Landing: conversion copywriter + UX writer + target customer + brand strategist.
- Email: email specialist (subject/open/click) + copywriter + spam analyst (deliverability) + target customer.
- Sales/long-form: direct response copywriter + skeptical buyer + editor + SEO specialist.

Moi persona cham 1–10 kem critique cu the (khong chi diem). Sua vung thap nhat truoc. Re-score. Tat ca ≥7, trung binh ≥8 moi giao.

Rubric: 9–10 publish-ready; 7–8 strong minor tweaks; 5–6 gaps ro can pass nua; 3–4 major revision; 1–2 rethink.

## 3. Quick-Pass — khi khong kip full 7 sweeps

Word-level: cat very/really/extremely/incredibly/just/actually/basically/in order to (→to)/that thua/things/stuff. Replace: utilize→use, implement→set up, leverage→use, facilitate→help, innovative→new, robust→strong, seamless→smooth, cutting-edge→new/modern. Watch: adverbs thua, passive → active, nominalizations (make a decision → decide).

Sentence: 1 y/cau, mix ngan dai, front-load quan trong, max 3 conjunctions, thuong ≤25 tu.

Paragraph: 1 topic/doan, 2–4 cau cho web, mo manh, flow logic, whitespace de scan.

## 4. 7 Benh pho bien & Thuoc

- Wall of Features → them "which means..." sau moi feature.
- Corporate Speak ("leverage synergies") → hoi "nguoi that noi sao?" roi dung tu do.
- Weak Opening (lich su cong ty/cau chung) → mo bang problem/outcome cua reader.
- Buried CTA → dua CTA ro + som + lap lai.
- No Proof → them testimonial/stat/case cu the.
- Generic Claims ("giup business grow") → cu the ai/how/bao nhieu.
- Mixed Audiences (noi moi nguoi = khong cham ai) → chon 1 audience viet thang.
- Feature Overload → giu 3–5 benefits quan trong nhat.

## 5. Checklist cuoi truoc khi giao

Chay im lang, hong muc nao sua roi moi giao:
- [ ] Core message giu nguyen, voice nhat quan
- [ ] Qua Rule of One + You Rule
- [ ] Moi claim co So What + Proof hoac da lam mem
- [ ] Khong vague words, co so that hoac chi tiet giac quan
- [ ] Co emotion texture dung cho, khong thao tung
- [ ] Gan CTA het friction, co risk reversal, next step ro
- [ ] Word/sentence/paragraph pass sach
- [ ] Bai quan trong: panel all ≥7, avg ≥8
- [ ] Dong bo voi copy-craft checklist (hook, tieu de, nao phai, brand test, CEP, tu rong, awareness, assets, khong bia, dung format)
