# Nghien cuu khach hang & Voice of Customer

Tong hop tu customer-research. Nhiem vu: moi claim trong content phai neo vao loi that khach noi, khong phai loi phong marketing tuong tuong.

## 1. Ba che do nghien cuu — xac dinh truoc khi lam

- Mode 1 Analyze Existing: da co transcript, survey, review, ticket. Viec la trich signal.
- Mode 2 Mine Online: gom intel tu Reddit, G2, forum, community — khach noi cong khai khong filter. Can biet tim o dau va trich gi.
- Mode 3 Go Ask: chua co signal, hoac cau hoi chi khach tra loi duoc. Chay interview + survey truc tiep.

Thuong phoi hop ca 3. Mine public truoc (Mode 2) de biet nen hoi gi va bang tu nao, roi moi hoi (Mode 3).

## 2. Mode 1 — Trich tu tai san co san

Transcript interview/sales call: pains, triggers, outcome muon, ngon ngu dung, objection, alternative da thu. Tim khoanh khac quyet dinh di tim giai phap, da thu gi truoc, thanh cong trong mat ho la gi.

Survey: segment theo tier/use case/tenure truoc khi ket luan. Open-ended quy hon multiple-choice (chung hay mau thuan nhau). Tim 20% response chua signal nhieu nhat.

Support ticket: phan loai truoc khi phan tich (bug vs confusion vs thieu feature vs lech ky vong). Mine "uoc gi no...".

Win/loss + churn notes: win thi giup giup chot, suy chon doi thu? loss/churn thi do gia/feature/fit/timing? Segment theo ly do, khong average.

NPS: passive + detractor signal manh hon promoter. Cap score voi verbatim — diem 9 kem complaint cu the quy hon diem 10 khong comment.

Khung trich 6 truong cho moi asset:
1. JTBD — functional job (viec can xong), emotional job (muon cam thay sao), social job (muon duoc nhin sao).
2. Pain Points — uu tien pain noi khong goi y + kem cam xuc.
3. Trigger Events — gi thay doi khien di tim giai phap (team tang, hire moi, miss target, su co xau ho, doi thu lam gi).
4. Desired Outcomes — thanh cong trong loi ho, trich nguyen van khong paraphrase.
5. Language — tu/cum that ("chung toi chet chim trong spreadsheet" quy hon "manual process inefficiency").
6. Alternatives Considered — doi thu, tu lam, thue nguoi, khong lam gi.

Tong hop: cluster theo theme → cham frequency × intensity → segment theo profile → chon 5–10 money quotes moi theme → flag mau thuan (noi 1 dang lam 1 neo).

Guardrails: gan confidence moi insight (High: 3+ nguon doc lap + unprompted + nhat quan; Medium: 2 nguon hoac prompted hoac 1 segment; Low: 1 nguon, can validate). Uu tien nguon 12 thang gan nhat. Check sample bias (review lech power user, ticket lech van de, Reddit lech technical/skeptical). Toi thieu 5 data points doc lap moi segment moi duoc dung persona/messaging.

## 3. Mode 2 — Digital Watering Hole

Chon nguon theo ICP: B2B SaaS/technical → Reddit role-subs, G2/Capterra, HN, LinkedIn, Indie Hackers, SparkToro. SMB/founder → r/entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness, Product Hunt, Facebook Groups. Developer → r/devops, r/programming, Stack Overflow, Discord. B2C → app reviews 1–3 sao, Reddit hobby subs, YouTube/TikTok comments. Enterprise → LinkedIn, analyst reports, G2 Enterprise filter, job postings.

Luat nhanh: co category → bat dau tu G2/Capterra (minh + doi thu). Muon biet audience o dau → SparkToro. Muon ngon ngu tho → Reddit + YouTube comments. Muon trigger → LinkedIn posts, job postings, Ask HN. Muon competitive intel → review 4 sao cua doi thu (khen + che chan that nhat).

Moi mau trich phai co: source (platform, URL, date) + verbatim quote + context + sentiment + theme tag (pain/trigger/outcome/alternative/language) + profile signals.

Tong hop thanh Top Themes rank theo frequency × intensity, moi theme co summary 1–2 cau + frequency + intensity + quotes nguyen van + implications cho messaging/product/positioning.

## 4. Mode 3 — Interview & Survey truc tiep

Quy tac dau: dung noi day la customer research — giu casual de khach noi that, khong dien. Nghien cuu la disconfirmation (chung minh minh sai) chu khong phai validation. Sales Safari cua Amy Hoy: nam vung noi audience tu tap (mine pains, jargon, recommendations, worldview) truoc khi hoi.

Tuyen best customers: segment CRM theo deal size / sales cycle ngan / churn thap; hoi sales & CS gioi thieu; ket moi cuoc bang "con ai nua chung toi nen noi chuyen?". Outreach email ngan, incentive $50/call, $5/survey. Muc tieu 10 calls, duoc 5 la mung.

Laddering 5 why: hoi why lien tuc de xuong root (vi du churn vi "dat" → why thay dat → why dung it → why team khong adopt → root la onboarding hong, khong phai gia).

PMF survey (Sean Ellis/Superhuman): "Ban se cam thay sao neu khong dung duoc [product] nua?" Benchmark 40% very disappointed (Superhuman dat 58%).

Phan tich ket qua tro lai bang khung Mode 1 + guardrails confidence.

## 5. Persona — chi dung khi du data

Khong co review thi di outward: differentiator cua minh (gia thuyet) → review doi thu truc tiep → review sp tuong tu tren marketplace → review brand lien ke cung audience. Gan tag proxy source, thay bang first-party khi co review that.

Khong dung persona truoc khi co 5–10 data points/segment. Cau truc: Profile (title range, company size, reports to) + Primary JTBD 1 cau + Triggers + Top Pains (bang loi ho) + Desired Outcomes (trong sao + do sao + trong the nao voi sep/team) + Objections/Fears + Alternatives + Key Vocabulary (nguyen van) + How to Reach (kenh, format, influencers/communities tin).

Anti-patterns: khong dat ten cute ("Marketing Mary") neu team khong can; khong average cross-segment; khong bia chi tiet — thieu thi de trong; revisit quarterly.

## 6. Deliverables tuy muc dich

Tuy nhu cau giao: research synthesis report / VOC quote bank theo theme (de viet copy) / persona 1–3 / JTBD map / competitive intel / research gap analysis (chua biet gi + tim sao). Hoi user can deliverable nao truoc khi xuat.

## 7. Cau noi vao content

VOC quote bank la dau vao truc tiep cho hook, tieu de, mo bai. Dung loi khach de goi ten con dau (copy-craft §2), dung trigger that de neo khoanh khac CEP (§8), dung objection that de viet RC10, dung outcome that de viet promise dung sophistication level. Brief thieu voc_assets thi insight la gia dinh — phai ghi ro va viet bang chi tiet giac quan thay vi so lieu.
