# Phan phoi, Social & Video — viet de lan toa

Tong hop tu: social, video, ad-creative. Dung o che do E va khi Content Type la social/video/ads. Bo sung cho format-playbooks.md (format-playbooks quy dinh cau phan bat buoc; file nay quy dinh cach viet de thang + cach tach atom + spec quang cao).

## 1. Platform Quick Ref

LinkedIn (B2B/thought leadership, 3–5x/tuan, carousel + story) / Twitter-X (tech/realtime, 3–10x/ngay, thread + hot takes) / Instagram (visual/lifestyle, 1–2 posts + stories/ngay, reels + carousel) / TikTok (awareness/tre, 1–4x/ngay, short video) / Facebook (community/local, 1–2x/ngay, group + native video).

## 2. Content Pillars Social 3–5 tru

Vi du SaaS founder: Industry insights 30% + Behind-the-scenes 25% + Educational 25% + Personal 15% + Promotional 5%. Moi pillar tra loi: perspective doc gi, audience hoi gi, gi da performance tot, lam deu duoc gi, khop business goal gi.

## 3. Hook Formulas — dong 1 quyet dinh tat ca

Curiosity: "I was wrong about [belief]." / "Ly do that cua [outcome] khong nhu ban nghi." / "[Ket qua] — chi mat [thoi gian ngan]."
Story: "Tuan truoc, [chuyen bat ngo] xay ra." / "Suyt nua thi [sai lam lon]." / "3 nam truoc toi [past], hom nay [present]."
Value: "How to [outcome] (without [pain]):" / "[Number] [things] that [outcome]:" / "Dung [loi pho bien]. Lam the nay:"
Contrarian: "Unpopular opinion: [bold]." / "[Loi khuyen pho bien] sai. Vi sao:" / "Toi bo [practice] va [ket qua]."

Carousel chon framework truoc khi viet slide: Value-Stack / Problem-Proof / Hack List / Rant Callout / Demo Walkthrough. Slide 1 hook ≤8 tu doc duoc o thumbnail; moi slide sau 1 y ≤25 tu, hieu duoc ca khi luot nhanh.

## 4. Repurposing — Content Atoms

Dung tao social tu zero. Tach tu pillar content thanh atoms dung doc lap: Quotable moment (bold claim 15–60s → X/LinkedIn/TikTok), Story arc (setup-conflict-resolution 60–90s → Reels/TikTok/Shorts), Tactical tip (how-to 30–60s → LinkedIn/Shorts), Controversial take (→ X/LinkedIn), Data callout (→ carousel/X), Behind-the-scenes (→ Stories/TikTok).

Workflow: Pillar (blog/video/podcast/webinar/newsletter) → trich 5–10 atoms (quotes, stories, tips, data) → adapt per platform (format/length/tone) → viet caption dung doc lap (khong gia dinh da doc goc) → schedule trai tuan → evergreen dang lai sau 3–6 thang.

Podcast 1 tap cho ra: 3–5 clip ngan 15–60s + 1–2 LinkedIn posts + 1 X thread + 1 carousel + 1 newsletter/blog tu doan hay nhat. Webinar cho ra: carousel tu slide chinh + thread Q&A + quote graphics + poll results + clip ngan. Newsletter cho ra: LinkedIn post tu insight chinh + X thread tu links + graphic tu stat + hot take rieng.

Podcast workflow: transcript (Whisper/Descript) → danh timestamp 5–10 moments → extract clips (Descript/Opus/CapCut) → viet caption co context → them subtitles (da so xem khong tieng) → schedule 1–2 tuan.

## 5. Calendar + Engagement + Scheduling

Weekly template: Mon insight/carousel, Tue behind-scenes/engagement/story, Wed educational/tips/reel, Thu story/thread/educational, Fri hot take/engagement/story. Batching 2–3h/tuan: review pillars → 5 LinkedIn → 3 threads + tweets → carousel + reel ideas → schedule → chua cho realtime.

Engagement 30p/ngay: rep all comments 5p + comment 5–10 target accounts 15p (them insight moi, share experience, hoi follow-up, disagree co nuance — cam "Great post!") + share co insight 5p + 2–3 DM 5p. Build 20–50 target accounts, engage deu roi collab.

Schedule: schedule core/threads/carousels/evergreen; post live realtime/news/engagement. Queue 1–2 tuan, review weekly, chua gap cho spontaneous. Analytics: awareness (impressions/reach/follower growth), engagement (rate/comments/shares/saves — comment quy hon like), conversion (clicks/profile visits/DMs/leads). Weekly review top3/bottom3 + trend + gio dang tot. Engagement thap → test hook/gio/format/engage nhieu hon. Reach giam → bo link ngoai body, tang frequency, them video.

Reverse-engineer viral: tim 10–20 creators engagement cao → gom 500+ posts → phan tich hooks/formats/CTAs → codify playbook → lap giong minh → bac cau ve business.

Luc tac: document journey, share dang hoc, curate + comment, engage manh. Luc stuck: repurpose bai win cu, hoi audience, comment news, share failure.

## 6. Short-Form Video — luat 3 giay

Specs: TikTok 15–60s 9:16 raw/authentic/trending sounds; Reels 15–30s polished/rewards saves; Shorts 30–60s + YouTube SEO title.

Moi video can 3 hooks dong thoi trong giay dau: VISUAL + VERBAL + TEXT OVERLAY.

Structures: Problem-Solution 15–30s (0–3 hook problem, 3–10 agitate, 10–25 solution, 25–30 CTA) / List 30–60s (hook "X things", moi item 5–8s, CTA) / Tutorial 30–60s (show end result truoc, overview, steps nhanh, result + CTA).

Caption/subtitle: MAX 2 dong, 3–5 tu/dong, sans-serif bold vien den, highlight keyword mau khac, khop timing. Tools CapCut/Descript/Captions.ai.

Y tuong theo type: SaaS (demo show outcome truoc, before/after, "watch me do X in Y seconds"), E-com (unbox, compare, how made, review), Services (process reveal, transformation, myth-bust), Personal brand (lessons, contrarian, day-in-life).

Loi: hook cham, khong text overlay, audio te, dai dong, khong CTA, bo comments gio dau.

## 7. Video Production — chon approach truoc khi quay

Programmatic (Hyperframes HTML/CSS — agent-friendly nhat, deterministic; Remotion React — animation phuc tap, batch Lambda) cho templated/data-driven/batch. AI Generation (Veo 3/Sora 2 top quality + audio; Kling/Seedance batch re; Hailuo consistency nhan vat; Hunyuan/Wan open self-host; Runway storyboard) cho B-roll/hero. AI Avatars (HeyGen lip-sync tot + MCP + custom twin 2–5p video; Synthesia corporate training) cho explainer/announcement/multilingual — khong dung cho founder authentic, UI walkthrough (dung screen record), creative (dung generation). Edit/Repurpose (Descript sua bang text, Opus auto-clip + virality score, CapCut polish, Captions.ai subs/dubbing).

Prompt video: subject + action + camera + style + mood. Cu the, co camera move (dolly/pan/static), co style (cinematic/documentary/commercial). Khong nho render text trong video AI — dung overlay programmatic.

Reverse-engineer edit viral: tach thanh edit spec beat-by-beat (cut rhythm, caption, punch-in, on-screen text, sound) roi ap vao footage minh. Copy grammar, khong copy footage/script/music.

## 8. Ad Creative — generate + iterate co grounding

4 modes: Generate from scratch / Iterate tu performance data (pull → pattern winners/losers → variations moi → validate specs) / Scaled Static Batches 50 concepts/batch tu grounded corpus + template library / Strategy Loop (quyet dinh lam gi truoc khi lam: tong hop account perf + customer language + organic external → rank concept → roadmap theo capacity → retro hang thang).

Grounded Inputs bat buoc cho scale: inputs/winning-ads (10–20 screenshots 90 ngay) + reviews (50–100) + comments (objection/praise tu ad comments — quy nhat, bo phi nhieu nhat) + brand voice/hex/logo/assets. Moi concept cite source. Khong bia claim/stat/testimonial. Inputs trong thi dung lai xin user, khong generate chay. Refresh winning-ads lien tuc, reviews/comments monthly.

Specs phai validate truoc khi giao: Google RSA headline 30 chars ×15 + desc 90 ×4 (headline dung doc lap moi to hop, co keyword + benefit + CTA, han che pin); Meta primary 125 visible (front-load hook) + headline 40 + desc 30; LinkedIn intro 150 (max 600) + headline 70 + desc 100; TikTok text 80–100 + name 40; X 280 + card headline 70 + desc 200.

Viet copy ads: dinh 3–5 angles khac motivation (pain, outcome, social proof, curiosity, comparison, urgency, identity, contrarian) truoc khi viet headline. Moi angle viet nhieu variations (word choice, specificity, tone, structure). Validate specs + flag over-limit kem ban cat gon. Output theo angle kem char counts; bulk 10+ thi kem CSV; batch static luu outputs/YYYY-MM-DD (INDEX.md + concepts/ + images/).

Chon format truoc khi brief: unicorn scaler (cold net-new: founder content, partnership ads, VSL, creator-fronted) vs supporting cast (mid-funnel). Static templates: Us vs Them, Stat Callout, Review Card, Before/After, Founder Message, FAQ Card (objection tu comments), Grid, Callout — cycle template, weight S/A tiers cho cold. Video: iMessage/ChatGPT/Notes/AirDrop reveals (hook screenshot → friend hoi → reveal + promo → end card; tuan thu illusion craft + grounding cho dramatized convo), faceless motion 15–45s (poster stills → image-to-video → TTS → word-timed captions, ~$3–6/15p), UGC/creator (reaction+demo hard cut, no-yapping split-screen, greenscreen, yapper, investigation, David & Goliath, authority, VSL, duet, ASMR, street interview) + vertical spec (safe-zone 720×1200, caption trang vien den khong pill, auto-size, quyet dinh organic vs baked music).

Iteration: phan tich winners (themes/structures/words/do dai) + losers (angles chet, generic/dai/sai tone) → double down winners bang phrasing moi + mo rong top 2 angles + test 1–2 angles moi + tranh pattern losers. Log Round/Date/Top-Winners/Patterns/New variations/Retired. Cho du 1000+ impressions truoc khi kill. Moi test cycle doi 1 bien.
