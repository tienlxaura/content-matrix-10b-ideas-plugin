# Chien luoc & Lap ke hoach Content

Tong hop tu: content-strategy, marketing-plan, marketing-ideas. Dung o che do D, va lam nen cho moi brief che do A.

## 1. Searchable vs Shareable — moi bai phai thuoc it nhat 1 loai

**Searchable** bat nhu cau co san. **Shareable** tao nhu cau moi. Uu tien searchable truoc vi traffic nen.

Searchable: nham 1 keyword/cau hoi, khop search intent chinh xac, title khop query, heading guong mau tim kiem, keyword o title/H1/doan dau/URL, bao phu toan dien khong bo sot cau hoi, co data + vi du + link uy tin, cau truc ro de AI/LLM trich dan (positioning ro, heading sach, brand nhat quan).

Shareable: insight moi / data goc / goc nhin nguoc so dong, phan bien co chung cu, story gay cam xuc, nguoi ta muon share de trong gioi hoac giup nguoi khac, bam trend / van de moi noi, chia se that bai mong de nguoi khac hoc.

## 2. Content Types theo muc dich

Searchable: Use-Case ([persona] + [use-case], vi du "quan ly task cho designer"), Hub and Spoke (hub tong quan + spokes chu de con, interlink; chi dung URL /guides khi co nhieu tang sau, con lai /blog la du), Template Libraries (danh tu khoa "marketing plan template", cho gia tri dung ngay, cho thay product nang template len).

Shareable: Thought Leadership (goi ten dieu ai cung cam ma chua ai noi, phan bien so dong co bang chung), Data-Driven (phan tich data san pham an danh, phan tich data cong khai, nghien cuu goc), Expert Roundups (15–30 chuyen gia tra loi 1 cau hoi cu the — co san distribution), Case Studies (Challenge → Solution → Results → Key learnings), Meta Content (hau truong minh bach).

Link-earning (khi muc tieu la backlink): uu tien Statistics/data roundups (~4.25x), Glossary (1.47x), Interactive tools/calculators (1.38x), How-to (1.36x). Nghien cuu goc tu lam chi 0.8x neu dung mot minh — phai kem trang stat-roundup tu tong hop lai duoi dang one-liner de hung link. Guide, template, thought leadership dung de rank + convert + brand, dung doi link tu chung.

## 3. Pillars & Topic Clusters 3–5 tru cot

Pillar tot: khop san pham, khop dieu audience quan tam, co search volume va/hoac social interest, du rong de de ra nhieu subtopic.

Tim pillars bang 4 huong: Product-led (san pham giai quyet gi), Audience-led (ICP can hoc gi), Search-led (topic nao co volume), Competitor-led (doi thu rank gi).

Cau truc: Pillar (Hub) → 2–3 Cluster → moi cluster 3 bai. Lien ket noi chien luoc giua cac bai cung cum.

## 4. Keyword theo Buyer Stage

| Stage | Modifiers | Vi du |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | what is, how to, guide to, introduction to | What is Agile, Guide to Sprint Planning |
| Consideration | best, top, vs, alternatives, comparison | Best PM tools for remote teams, Asana vs Trello |
| Decision | pricing, reviews, demo, trial, buy | Pricing comparison, How to choose plan |
| Implementation | templates, examples, tutorial, setup, how to use | Template library, Step-by-step setup |

## 5. Nguon y tuong — 6 kenh khai thac

1. Keyword exports (Ahrefs/SEMrush/GSC): gom cum, gan buyer stage + intent, tim quick win (volume kha + competition thap + relevance cao), tim gap doi thu rank ma minh khong. Output bang Keyword/Volume/Difficulty/Stage/Type/Priority.
2. Call transcripts: cau hoi → FAQ/blog; pain → dung nguyen van; objection → content xu ly truoc; ngon ngu → VOC; doi thu duoc nhac → goc so sanh.
3. Survey: open-ended quy hon multiple-choice; theme ≥30% la uu tien cao; resource request; format preference.
4. Forum (Reddit site:reddit.com + topic, Quora, Indie Hackers, HN, Slack/Discord): FAQ, hieu lam, tranh cai, thuat ngu that.
5. Competitor (site:competitor.com/blog): bai performance cao, topic lap lai, gap chua cover, case study, cau truc pillar.
6. Sales + Support: objection lap lai, cau hoi lap lai, ticket pattern, success story, feature request va van de dang sau no.

## 4b. Thu vien 139 y tuong — dung de pha bi tac

Khi brief bi tac hoac can mo rong ngoai 10 huong Matrix, rut tu 16 nhom: Content & SEO 1–10, Competitor 11–13, Free Tools 14–22, Paid Ads 23–34, Social & Community 35–44, Email 45–53, Partnerships 54–64, Events 65–72, PR & Media 73–76, Launches 77–86, Product-Led 87–96, Content Formats 97–109, Unconventional 110–122 (bao gom guerrilla: relevance + relationship + precision targeting, test nho truoc khi bung), Platforms 123–130, International 131–132, Developer 133–136, Audience-Specific 137–139.

Chon theo stage (pre-launch: waitlist referral; early: content + community + founder sales; growth: paid + partnership + events; scale: brand + international), theo budget (free: content/community/social; low: targeted ads/sponsorship/free tools; medium: events/partnership/PR; high: acquisition/conference/brand), theo timeline (quick win: ads/email/social; medium: content/SEO/community; long: brand/thought leadership/platform).

Moi y tuong de xuat phai co: ten + vi sao hop + 2–3 buoc bat dau + outcome + resource can.

## 6. Cham diem uu tien — 40/30/20/10

Customer Impact 40% (tan suat, % khach gap, cam xuc, LTV) + Content-Market Fit 30% (khop van de product giai, insight doc, co story, dan tu nhien ve product) + Search Potential 20% (volume, competition, long-tail, trend) + Resources 10% (expertise, research can them, assets). Cham 1–10 moi cot, nhan trong so, rank, lam tu cao xuong.

## 7. Calendar 60/30/10 + Ky luat per-format

60% searchable (nen), 30% shareable (links/mentions), 10% thu nghiem (format/kenh/cuoc moi). Brand moi nang searchable; brand manh day shareable.

Ky luat san xuat: Blog — viet 10 title truoc khi draft, ~5 editing passes. Guide dai — flagship cua pillar, co TOC + link ve spokes, lam hub truoc. Video — script hook truoc, front-load payoff, cat san clip ngan ngay luc quay. Podcast — 1 tap phai tach san transcript + quote graphic + clip + recap. Email — 1 y tuong/send, subject viet nhieu chon 1.

## 8. Create Once Distribute Twice + ORB

Mot flagship xuat sac tai su dung khap noi hon nhieu bai trung binh moi noi 1 bai. Thiet ke hook phan phoi ngay khi viet: subhead tach duoc thanh post, section boc duoc modular, quote/stat lam san graphic.

Pheu ORB: Borrowed (podcast khach, guest post, partnership — discovery) → Rented (social, ad network — engagement, khong so huu) → Owned (email list, blog, community — conversion, tai san ben vung). Moi thu upstream phai do ve owned.

That bai can tranh: spray-and-pray (dang khap noi khong flagship), platform dependency (organic reach co the rot tu 20% ve duoi 2% qua dem), ownership paradox (do 90% suc vao kenh khong so huu, bo be owned).

## 9. Khung AARRR cho ke hoach lon

Acquisition (la → biet) / Activation (biet → trai nghiem dau tien convert: signup, onboarding, first session) / Retention (o lai + sau hon: lifecycle, churn prevention) / Referral (dan nguoi moi: ambassador/affiliate/WOM) / Revenue (pricing, upsell, bundle, ACV). Brand + content xuyen suot, khong phai stage rieng.

Marketing nhu dau tu: compounding portfolio chu khong phai lich campaign. Khong silver bullet, chi co tram golden pellets. Mot asset phai cho nhieu returns (rank + backlink + newsletter + social + talk). Audition not auction — xung dang duoc chu y chu khong mua khan gia. Hope is not strategy — moi muc co mechanism + leading indicator + kill criterion neu la experiment.

Market-quality gate truoc khi plan: cham problem Size × Frequency. Lon × thuong xuyen = nen tot nhat de compounding. Nho × hiem = yeu nhat, phai noi thang vi no that CAC va channel mix.

Roadmap 90 ngay: tuan 1–2 Unblock, 3–4 Foundation, 5–8 Velocity, 9–12 Compound. Gan owner + gan tag AARRR tung muc. Outlook 12 thang theo quy, gan voi funding milestones mo khoa nang luc.

## 10. Output ke hoach chuan

Pillars (3–5 + rationale + subtopic + noi ve product) + Priority Topics (title, searchable/shareable, type, keyword, buyer stage, vi sao — backed by research) + Topic Cluster Map (so do lien ket) + Lich (30/90 ngay) + Do luong (north-star + leading indicator per stage).
