# Tam ly hoc thuyet phuc dung cho Content

Tong hop tu marketing-psychology. Nguyen tac: moi bai chi chon 1–3 trigger chu dao. Nhieu hon se loan. Tat ca phai dung mot cach ethical — chi dung scarcity/urgency that, guarantee trong kha nang, khong thao tung.

## 1. Tu duy nen — dung de chon huong chien luoc

- First Principles: hoi why 5 lan ve goc de tim root cause, dung copy doi thu vi doi thu lam.
- Jobs to Be Done: nguoi ta "thue" san pham de hoan thanh tien bo trong hoan canh cu the. Viet ve job, khong viet ve spec. Day la cau noi MP04.
- Circle of Competence: khong dan trai moi kenh. Danh manh noi co loi the that.
- Inversion: liet ke moi thu khien bai that bai (nham nguoi, mo bai sai awareness, CTA mo) roi chan tung cai.
- Occam's Razor: conversion rot thi check form hong, toc do trang truoc khi do cho attribution phuc tap.
- Pareto 80/20: tim 20% kenh/khach/content gay 80% ket qua. Cat phan con lai.
- Local vs Global Optima: toi uu subject line (local) vo nghia neu email khong phai kenh dung (global). Zoom out truoc.
- Theory of Constraints: chi co 1 nut that co chai tai 1 thoi diem. Traffic thap thi dung toi uu conversion.
- Opportunity Cost + Diminishing Returns: bai thu 10 khong bang bai dau. Biet khi nao da dang hoa thay vi gap doi.
- Second-Order Thinking: sale dot tang revenue (order 1) nhung day khach doi giam gia (order 2).
- Map ≠ Territory: persona la mo hinh, khach that phuc tap hon. Cham khach that thuong xuyen.
- Probabilistic + Barbell: 80% budget kenh proven, 20% bet mao hiem. Khong danh cuc vao 1 campaign.

## 2. Hieu nguoi mua — dung de viet insight va mo bai

- Fundamental Attribution Error: khach khong convert thuong do quy trinh roi, khong phai do ho khong nghiem tuc.
- Mere Exposure: lap lai nhat quan gay thich. Can rule hien dien da kenh.
- Availability Heuristic: case study + testimonial khien thanh cong de tuong tuong hon.
- Confirmation Bias: hieu niem tin san co cua audience, dung danh thang vao niem tin.
- Lindy Effect: nguyen tac lau doi (value prop ro, social proof) song lau hon trick moi.
- Mimetic Desire: nguoi ta muon thu nguoi dang muon muon. Waitlist, exclusivity, social proof kich hoat.
- Sunk Cost: biet khi nao kill campaign yeu. Tien da do khong bien minh cho tien sap do.
- Endowment + IKEA Effect: trial/sample/freemium cho cam giac so huu; cho tu customize/cau hinh tang perceived value.
- Zero-Price Effect: free khac han gia thap. Nhay tu $1 ve $0 lon hon tu $2 ve $1.
- Present Bias: nhan manh loi ich ngay ("bat dau tiet kiem tu hom nay") hon ROI 6 thang.
- Status-Quo Bias + Default Effect: giam friction chuyen doi ("import 1 click"), pre-select goi muon ban.
- Paradox of Choice + Hick's Law: 3 pricing tiers thang 7. 1 CTA ro thang 3 CTA.
- Goal-Gradient + Zeigarnik: progress bar, "ban da 80%", vong lap do khien nguoi ta muon hoan thanh.
- Peak-End Rule: thiet ke dinh dang nho + ket manh (thank-you page, follow-up email).
- Pratfall Effect: thua nhan nhuoc diem nho ("chung toi khong re nhat, nhung...") tang trust.
- Curse of Knowledge: cai hien nhien voi ban thi mo ho voi newbie. Test copy voi nguoi ngoai nganh.
- Mental Accounting: "$3/ngay" khac "$90/thang" du bang nhau. Chon mental account co loi.
- Regret Aversion + Loss Aversion: mat mat dau gap doi duoc. "Dung bo lo" thang "ban co the dat". Giam so hoi tiec bang guarantee, trial, no commitment.
- Bandwagon / Social Proof: so luong khach, logo, review, trending. So tao confidence.

## 3. Thuyet phuc hanh vi — dung de viet CTA va than bai

- Reciprocity: cho truoc (content, tool, free tier) roi moi hoi.
- Commitment & Consistency + Foot-in-the-Door: cam ket nho truoc (signup, trial) roi leo thang (paid → annual → enterprise).
- Authority Bias: expert endorsement, chung chi, featured-in logos, thought leadership.
- Liking / Similarity + Unity: nguoi dai dien relatable, founder story, ngon ngu cong dong, "built by X for X", shared identity.
- Scarcity / Urgency: chi dung khi that (slot that, deadline that, stock that). Gia tao = sup do trust dai han.
- Door-in-the-Face: show gia enterprise truoc roi reveal starter — contrast khien starter thay re.
- Anchoring + Decoy: neo so dau tien (gia goc, gia doi thu, tier cao) truoc; them decoy tier kem ro de tier muon ban thang.
- Framing + Contrast: "90% success" khac "10% failure" du giong nhau. Show before ro thi after moi vivid.
- AIDA: Attention → Interest → Desire → Action. Bat attention truoc khi xay desire.
- Rule of 7: can ~7 touchpoints truoc khi convert. Retargeting + sequence + hien dien deu cong don.
- Nudge / EAST / COM-B / BJ Fogg: hanh vi = Motivation × Ability × Prompt (thieu 1 la that). Lam cho Easy, Attractive, Social, Timely. Check Capability + Opportunity + Motivation.
- Activation Energy: buoc dau phai de den muc buon cuoi (pre-fill form, template, quick win 2 phut).

## 4. Tam ly gia — dung khi viet offer/pricing/CTA

- Charm Pricing: .99 cho value; tron so cho premium ($500 cam cao cap hon $497).
- Rule of 100: gia duoi $100 dung % off ("20% off" thang "$16 off"); gia tren $100 dung so tuyet doi ("$100 off" thang "20% off").
- Good-Better-Best: 3 tiers, middle la target. Tier dat lam neo, tier re lam moc.
- Reframe gia theo ngay: "bang coc ca phe sang" de dua vao mental account re.

## 6. Luat dao duc bat buoc

- Khong scarcity gia (countdown gia, "chi con 3 slot" gia). Lift ngan han, sup trust dai han.
- Khong guarantee qua suc ("x2 revenue hoac refund + $1000"). Case fail hiem se no public.
- Khong bonus inflation ($50K bonus cho sp $497). Buyer tinh thay ngay.
- Khong course-bro aesthetic cho sp nghiem tuc (secret method, gold logo, urgency gia).
- Khong discount de acquire khach moi (coupon churn gap ~2x, neo re). Discount chi cho upgrade/cross-sell hoac mua that. Tang value bang offer thay vi giam gia.
